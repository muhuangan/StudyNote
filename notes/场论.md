# 场论

梯度:

$$
\mathrm{grad}\,f=\nabla f=(f_x,f_y,f_z)
$$

散度:

$$
\mathrm{div}\,\vec F=\nabla\cdot\vec F
=\frac{\partial P}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial Q}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial R}{\partial z}
$$

旋度:

$$
\mathrm{rot}\,\vec F=\nabla\times\vec F=
\left(
\frac{\partial R}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial Q}{\partial z},
\frac{\partial P}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial R}{\partial x},
\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}
\right)
$$

拉普拉斯算子:

$$
\Delta f=\mathrm{div}(\mathrm{grad}\,f)=f_{xx}+f_{yy}+f_{zz}
$$

保守场:

若 $\mathrm{rot}\,\vec F=0$, 且在单连通区域上, 则:

- 曲线积分与路径无关;
- 存在势函数 $u$, 使 $\vec F=\nabla u$;
- 闭曲线积分为 $0$.
