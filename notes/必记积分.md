# 必记积分

$$
\int\frac{dx}{a^2 + x^2},\quad \int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}},\quad \int\frac{dx}{x^2 - a^2}
$$

$$
\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2\pm a^2}}
$$
